# Project notes (Setuk website)

- Read `README.md` first for the architecture: two root layouts (`app/(en)`, `app/(hi)`), typed dictionaries in `content/`, and a framework-free Three.js engine in `lib/sadan/` that is lazy-loaded by `components/home/story/story-controller.tsx`.
- All copy lives in `content/en.ts` and `content/hi.ts`, both typed by `content/types.ts`. Don't hard-code user-facing strings in components.
- Use the Lok Navy tokens (`bg-bg`, `bg-surface`, `text-ink`, `text-muted`, `border-line`, `bg-accent`, `text-accent-ink`, `bg-accent-soft`), not raw colours. Blue (`accent`) is the primary; `leaf` (`text-leaf`, `bg-leaf`, `text-leaf-ink`, `bg-leaf-soft`) is the logo green, a second accent kept to small touches (outcomes, checks, the recommendation, WhatsApp, ornaments), never buttons or headlines.
- Import icons only through `components/icons.ts`.
- Layout modes: `classic`, `band` and `side` are chosen by media queries in `components/home/story/story.module.css` (exposed as `--story-mode`). Keep the queries in step with the compact-mode checks in `story-controller.tsx` and `app/globals.css`. Compact framing for a stop lives in its `c` entry in `STOPS`. Never hide copy on small screens.
- Story cards (`chapters.tsx`) and camera stops (`STOPS` in `lib/sadan/engine.ts`) are paired by index. The office scenes (chapters 2, 3 and 5 to 8) are driven by `update()` in `lib/sadan/office-set.ts` from the same progress value; the Run close-up feeds the camera through `office.shot`.
- Strings drawn in the 3D scene live in `scene` in both dictionaries (`SceneLabels` in `content/types.ts`).
- Inner pages: build links with `href(locale, path)` and `routes` from `lib/paths.ts`; page copy goes in `pages` in both dictionaries. Shapes on inner pages: pictures and cards `rounded-3xl`, tiles and inputs `rounded-xl`, buttons full pills. Use `Figure` (or the `illo` class) for Setuk's illustrations so dark mode dims them. Sections ease in with the CSS-only `reveal` class; keep motion to that, the hero `rise`, the CSS-only routes backdrop in `PageHero` (`components/site/routes.tsx`), the role heroes' case card (it follows the hairline tour and plays once), and the one GSAP rail on How we work.
- Demo buttons: every "Request demo" link keeps its `href` to the contact page (works without script) and carries `data-demo` (`PrimaryLink` takes `demo`); a click opens the one request-a-demo dialog, `DemoHost` in `components/site/demo-dialog.tsx`, mounted by `SiteDocument`. `data-demo="0".."3"` preselects a role in `ROLE_KEYS` order. Copy lives in `demo` in both dictionaries. Give new demo buttons `data-demo` rather than a plain contact link.
- Articles and policies are English only; wrap their text in `lang="en"` on `/hi` pages so the Hindi type rules skip it.
- Decorative patterns: use `Pattern`, `CornerPattern` and `Lotus` from `components/site/pattern.tsx` with `text-*` and `opacity-*` classes. Don't add raw SVG backgrounds.
- Inner pages carry the homepage's ornament through `components/pages/ui.tsx`: `PageHero` (kicker, sadan wall, animated routes), `Band` (`surface`, `tint`, `ink`), `SectionHead` (a `Kicker` over the H2; kicker copy lives beside each section's title), `Panel` (the FAQ panel) and `IconTile`. Use them for new sections instead of styling one-off; keep to one feature band (`tint` or `ink`) per page. Use `overflow-clip`, not `overflow-hidden`, on anything that wraps a sticky element.
- Anything new in the set that animates must keep `animating` honest, so the engine can idle and cap frames.
- Textures under `public/sadan/v1/` are cached as immutable. Put changed files in a new version folder.
- Voter list in Excel (one optional catch-all route per locale) keeps the live site's addresses: `/[state]`, its lists `/[state]/all-districts`, `all-assembly-constituencies` and `all-parliamentary-constituencies`, `/[state]/[pc]`, `/[state]/[district]` and `/[state]/[district]/[ac]` (the full list is in `lib/voter-list/types.ts`). Every page gets its data from `getVoterListView` in `lib/voter-list/api.ts`, which reads `lib/voter-list/mock.ts` for now; the mock is built on the live site's catalogue in `lib/voter-list/data/` (`catalogue.json` for areas and figures, `copy.json` for what editors wrote), refreshed with `scripts/voter-list/export.sh`. The shapes in `lib/voter-list/types.ts` are the contract with the backend; to connect the real API, change only `api.ts`. Area names and write-ups are English data; the labels around them live in `pages.voterList`. A state's list can hold hundreds of cards, so keep per-card markup lean: cards share one order dialog (`OrderHost`) and one icon sprite. The card is `AreaCard` in `components/pages/voter-list-card.tsx`, shared by server and client: the server draws the first 12 cards, and a longer list goes on as data in `VoterListMore` (`voter-list-more.tsx`, virtualised by rows with `@tanstack/react-virtual`); the index at the foot keeps every area's link in the HTML. Cut each card's map on the server (`artOf`) so the shapes data never reaches the client. State cards carry the state's outline, and district, constituency and Lok Sabha cards their state with their own area lit, from `lib/voter-list/shapes.ts` (drawn by `scripts/voter-list/state-shapes.py` from an MIT dataset that follows India's official boundaries; within a state the outline is drawn once, in the page's sprite). Assam and Jammu and Kashmir light only their districts, drawn from each state's district file: their seats were redrawn after the boundary data was made (and in Assam, Barpeta, Bajali, Baksa and Tamulpur show no area, Bajali and Tamulpur having been split off since). Any new map of India must follow the Survey of India's boundaries too.
- Product pages (`/office-management-software`, `/election-management-software`, in `routes.products`): one layout in `components/pages/products/product-page.tsx`, copy in `pages.products` (typed by `ProductCopy`), identity (hero pattern, menu icon, module and feature icons by index) in `products/look.ts`. Each hero draws its product screen in HTML from `screen` (`screens.tsx`), with illustrative data labelled as such; keep any new screen element decorative inside the `role="img"` wrapper and position floating cards with top/left, since `rise` owns transform. Module lists and live/in-progress badges must match How we work. `/political-office-management-software` stays a redirect to its article (next.config.ts), so don't reuse that path. The header's Products and By role menus are both `NavMenu` in `site-header.tsx`.
- Role pages (`/for-mps`, `/for-mlas`, `/for-mayors`, `/for-consultants`): sections in `components/pages/roles/`, copy in `pages.roles`, identity (figure, window, tour stops, pattern, menu icon) in `roles/look.ts`. Each hero has its own composition (`role-hero.tsx`); its case card (`role-stage.tsx`, copy in each role's `case`) must stay readable without motion, and the stops must land on the parts the case names. Each hero figure is a hairline figure in `public/hairline/v1/`; make or change one only with the hairline-create skill and its `look.mjs`, never edit `kernel.js`, and put changed figures in a new version folder. Describe only modules that are live on How we work; badge the rest "In progress". Kickers on about one section in three.
