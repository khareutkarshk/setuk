Section 01

1Introduction and Objective

The Office of the District Magistrate receives a large volume of correspondence from citizens, public representatives and government departments. At present, each communication is examined, forwarded to the concerned department and followed up manually. This results in avoidable delay, difficulty in tracing the status of a communication and limited supervisory visibility.

From files followed up by hand to a single traceable record for every letter.

Setuk Pvt. Ltd. proposes a web-based application through which every letter received by the office is uploaded, interpreted, assigned to the competent officer and tracked until disposal. The system will also place delayed and problematic matters before the District Magistrate, accompanied by a decision-support note setting out possible courses of action.

The objectives of the system are

01To ensure that every letter received is registered, assigned and traceable.
02To ensure that each letter is routed to the appropriate officer without manual triage.
03To monitor compliance against prescribed timelines (SLA) and highlight non-compliance.
04To assist the District Magistrate in deciding the way forward on matters that remain unresolved.

Section 02

2Proposed Workflow

Seven steps take a letter from the receiving desk to disposal. Each step can run with staff approval or, where the office so decides, automatically.

01

Upload

Office staff

Authorised office staff upload the scanned or photographed letter, whether handwritten or typed, in Hindi, Punjabi, English or another Indian language.

02

Extraction

OCR + AI

The system extracts the text and the principal particulars of the letter, namely the sender, date, subject, location and the relief sought. It identifies whether the letter is new, or a repeat or revision of an earlier communication and links it to the existing case.

03

Assignment

Confirm or automatic

On the basis of the officer directory furnished by the office (name, designation, department, email ID and SLA days), the system identifies the competent officer and records the reasoning. The dealing staff confirm or modify the assignment. Alternatively, the office may set the assignment to automatic, in which case the email is dispatched without staff confirmation.

04

Dispatch

Email

The system sends an email to the assigned officer from the office's designated email address. The email contains the letter, a summary, the due date (SLA days from the date of dispatch) and a unique reference number in the subject line.

05

Receipt of reply

Mapped by reference

The officer replies to the same email, or sends a fresh email or letter quoting the reference number. The system receives the communication, maps it to the case and appends it to the case file.

06

Status determination

Automatic

Where the reply states that the work is complete, the case is marked Solved. Where the reply states that the work cannot be done, the case is marked Issue Reported by Department and the reason given is recorded. Where no reply is received within the SLA period, the case is marked SLA Failed.

07

Advisory

For the District Magistrate

For each case marked Issue Reported by Department or SLA Failed, the District Magistrate is presented with the case history, the possible options for resolution and examples of how comparable matters have been resolved elsewhere.

Section 04

4Functional Description

4.1Letter Intake and Optical Character Recognition

  • Upload of one or more files, in the form of scans, photographs or PDF files, relating to a single case. The letter and its attachments are saved separately and each attachment remains linked to the case.
  • Text extraction from handwritten and printed letters using Chandra 2.0, an OCR model, followed by LLM-based structuring of the extracted particulars.
  • Side-by-side display of the original image and the extracted text, permitting correction by staff before assignment. This review may be set to automatic, in which case the extracted particulars are accepted without staff review.

4.2Assignment and Email Dispatch

  • The officer directory (name, designation, department, email ID and SLA days) is maintained by the office and may be updated through the application.
  • The system proposes an officer; the dealing staff confirm the assignment prior to dispatch. This step may be set to automatic by the office, in which case the email is dispatched on the system's own assignment.
  • Outgoing emails follow a standard format and carry the reference number and the due date.

4.3Reply Tracking and Mapping

  • A dedicated office mailbox is monitored continuously.
  • Replies are mapped primarily through the reference number. Where no reference number is quoted, mapping is attempted using the sender, the email thread and the content of the letter. Doubtful matches are placed in a queue for staff verification and are not mapped automatically.
  • All emails, letters and notes relating to a case are maintained in a single chronological case file.

4.4Dashboard

The dashboard presents three principal views to the District Magistrate:

View 1

Solved Cases

Matters in which the department has confirmed completion, with the reply and any supporting document attached.

View 2

SLA Failed Cases

Matters in which no reply has been received within the prescribed period, in order of delay.

View 3

Issues Reported by Department

Matters in which the department has stated that the work cannot be done, together with the reason given.

  • Summary counts of cases by status, with filters for department, officer, date and area.
  • Reminders to officers before expiry of the SLA period and escalation upon expiry.

4.5Advisory Support to the District Magistrate

For each case in the SLA Failed and Issues Reported by Department views, the system will prepare an Advisory Note setting out what can be done and what is being done elsewhere in the world on similar issues. The note contains:

A · Problem

A concise statement of the problem and the reason furnished by the department.

B · Options

Reassignment to another department, escalation to a higher authority, an alternative source of funds, a joint inspection, or a formal reminder.

C · Rules check

A check of each option against the Government Orders, Acts and circulars loaded into the system, with the source cited.

D · Precedents

How similar issues have been addressed in other districts, States and countries, each with a reference to its source.

Note. The Advisory Note is intended solely to support decision-making. The decision rests with the District Magistrate. Each suggestion will indicate its source and any item that could not be verified will be marked as such. The quality of the advisory depends on the legal and precedent material made available to the system.

Section 08

8Security and Oversight

Data will be encrypted in storage and in transit and hosted within India.
Personal details such as telephone numbers and addresses may be masked before text is sent to an external AI service. The choice of AI provider and its data terms will be agreed with the office.
Each of the review, assignment and dispatch steps may be operated with staff approval or in automatic mode, as decided by the office. Statuses are open to review and correction by the District Magistrate and authorised staff, and all such changes are logged.
A complete audit log will be maintained of the actions of users and the suggestions made by the AI.
