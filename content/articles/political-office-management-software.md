## Introduction

If you ever been into any political office or ran the office then you know how many people come daily with applications, complaints, requests, invitation and expectations. Managing those while reply on paper files, whatsapp groups, excel sheets, phone calls and personal memory is not just hard, it's impossible. There are two ways to solve this issue either you need a superhuman at your office or a Digital Political Office Management Software.

## What is Political Office Management Software?

A political office is not like a Business office selling something or providing a predictable set of services to their customer. This office serves citizens and every citizen comes up with a different issue, complaint, invitation, request and demand or they can simply wish to meet the their representative in person.

Everyday, an MP, MLA, Minister, Mayor or other public representative's office get hundreds of such requests from citizens. Someone at the office needs to record everyone who came, what they want, what was promised and what to do now on it. Someone has to ensure that the citizen shouldn't be forgotten just when they leave the office.

These are the daily work which happens in any political office:

- Meeting with citizens and understanding their requests
- Recording their applications and complaints
- Letter drafting and dispatching
- Scheduling appointments with the representative
- Managing Public Programs and Events
- Connecting and Designating task to karyakartas (volunteers)
- Tracking work done on made promises
- Informing the representative on required topics

In India, most of the such political offices do all this without any dedicated system. Meetings are managed on a register or personal notebook. Documents and images are sent on whatsapp manually. Doing rest of the daily work depends upon the staffs priority or mood that day.

If the citizen counts increases then this way of managing office will break. Then there will be Meetings just in register, impossible to search whatsapp chats and no one can say how many requests are pending or how many urgent work is hidden under the pile of letters.

The Political Office Management Software solves exactly this problem. It helps you to look into every citizen interaction, letters, appointments and program in a digital system where you can see who asked for what, what needs to be done on it and by when.

## Why Political Offices Need Digital Management

### Citizen expectations are rising

Today, Citizens won't be satisfied by just that their representative heard the issue. They need a tracking system and feedback mechanism. They need updates when their letter went into process, what someone is doing on it and when they should expect a response because they are in habit of doing the same with a bank or online order. Any political office who cannot give these basic experience and assurance loses trust and that lost trust might turn into lost votes.

### The workload keeps growing

Any representative or politician want their reach among citizens grow and along with this more people will reach their office with requests or complaints. An office which was working efficiently with a notebook and whatsapp, will eventually fail with increasing number of citizens.

### Offices are rarely in one place

Do you how many offices a representative have? An office in his constituency, another at the capital, one at the party office, if representative is an MP then one in each legislative assembly, some are set up during election or programs. Each of these offices have letters, appointment details and citizen records spread across and bringing them all together is not easy.

### Paper records don't scale

A register works if there are ten to twenty entries a day but once it becomes hundred then it is unreliable. Pages are torn off, pen ink fades or spreads, sometimes handwriting is unreadable, once the register pages are all filled then no one opens it again and top of that searching a specific citizen's interaction means going through every page one by one.

### WhatsApp was never built for office work

Whatapp is built for communication between people but never designed to keep records of anyone. Sharing documents is still ok but thinking you will find the documents from chat when you need it is just a matter of luck. Chats are never built with structure, no label and you can never know for whom you need to take action.

### Excel sheets break down quickly

Excel spreadsheets is a good option but only for a while, once the different staffs of the office starts updating it. There is no history of update, anyone can overwrite anything or everything, formula breaks as input type is not defined and documents cannot be added to it.

### No visibility into what's happening

Working offline means there is no dashboard, no numbers to glace or nothing flagged as priority to do. Every time, representative have to ask the staff to know what's pending and office is working on what.

### No accountability for who did what

If a citizen gives his request even as a letter but when it's not marked in proper channels. Probably no one is going to act on it or at least there is no accountability to act on it. And when the citizen comes back to enquire about it later, then there is no record of where that letter is and what work is done upon it.

### No data to learn from

An office with Political Office Management tool learns from its data, which area have more complaints, which type of complaints are frequent, which area have least interaction with representatives or who all met the representatives till date. But an office which runs offline on paper or whatsapp cannot do all this.

## Common Challenges Political Offices Face

### Citizen requests scattered across channels

Citizens send their applications by hand, via post, via whatsapp and also through field volunteers, with these many channels then applications get lost among them when there is no single place to log them.

### Letters are difficult to track

If a letter sent to government department regarding any application then it can take weeks or sometime even months to get a reply from them. Sometimes, offices need to send another follow-up letter to enquire about the application but without a political office management software it's very hard to track it.

### Appointments overlap

Citizens come to the office, just to find that representative is unavailable at the office or have to wait for a long period of time because many were given same time for the meeting. A digital solution made specifically for politicians can solve it.

### Programs are poorly coordinated

Imagine, managing invitations, attendee lists, logistics, follow-up and other important things of a public programs on notebook or multiple phones and whatsapp chats. When there are different people managing different roles in many different programs, this will be definitely result in a poorly coordinated programs.

### Team communication gaps

A representative have multiple offices, staffs and team at those offices, volunteers on field and representative work from different locations. They need a shared system which is online, real-time updated, information and documents reach to only those who needs it and task tracking. Not a whatsapp group with 25 people and thousands of message everyday.

### Paper records pile up

Registers, notebooks and file take physical space and once the office is operating from few years then it is hard or next to impossible search anything.

### No reminders

Daily work and priority of work will depends on what is going on that day or how much work load is there. Follow-ups solely depend on someone's memory.

### No reports

Working offline with paper or WhatsApp groups will not present a report and it is a time taking to create a summary from these. While a digital software can do this every easily and efficiently.

### No historical data

You might remember a face for few days, weeks or even a month but a citizen who returns after a year with a application related to their earlier request. Office is unable to find previous records of that citizen interaction and need to start from scratch.
